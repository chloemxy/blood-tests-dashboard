What this atlas doesn't know yet

216 entries are quoted verbatim from a named authority — MedlinePlus and MedlinePlus Genetics (NIH), NCBI, the National Cancer Institute, and the ARUP, Mayo, Labcorp and Quest test directories — with a link so you can check. The other 5,976 were written by an AI model from the test's official LOINC name and category, and every one links to its LOINC record. Four of seventeen families have no quoted entry at all; the largest family, infectious disease, has 5 quoted out of 1,903. We show you which is which on every row, because the difference matters more than the total.
