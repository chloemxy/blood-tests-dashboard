about

A model of the blood system

Standard lab testing can miss the personalized factors that actually shape care, and researching them on your own tends to mean either too much information or too little — it's rarely clear what's connected, what matters now, and what can wait.

This atlas starts from results you already have. Marking a marker low or high shows what that result can point at, and which of those things your panel could not have reached. An unmeasured marker is not automatically a gap — the real question is what additional information would actually change the interpretation, and when enough is already known.

The concept behind it goes further than this build does today: eventually weighing symptoms, history, medications and prior trends, and reaching past a single blood draw into continuous data like wearables. The endpoint isn't “order this test” — it's helping you understand what a result means, whether follow-up is worth raising, and what's worth asking a clinician. It does not diagnose, does not recommend tests, and does not know your medical history.
